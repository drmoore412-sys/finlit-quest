## 3. New governed terms — IMPLEMENTED / TESTED

| Term | ID | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| ACH | `banking.ach` | An electronic network that moves money between bank and credit union accounts, used for direct deposits and recurring bill payments. |
| WORTH | `investing.worth` | The amount of money something is valued at, or the amount by which what a person owns is greater than what they owe, as in net worth. |

- WORTH's definition describes **monetary value**, as you required, and does not describe an investment instrument (a test rejects the words investment, security, stock and fund in it).
- Sources and verification notes (CFPB and Federal Reserve for ACH; Investor.gov and the SEC for WORTH) are recorded in `content/v3-supplemental-terms.json`.
- **Design choice:** the frozen Banking v1.1 record is byte-pinned and its 26 terms are covered by 13 two-word clusters, so ACH cannot be added to those files without breaking the legacy puzzle bank. The two terms live in a **governed V3 supplement** (`content/v3-supplemental-terms.js` / `.json`), which the V3 slate merges internally. The legacy Banking (26) and Investing (25) data and banks are untouched, with a test proving it. The approved vocabulary is now **147 terms** (145 original + 2).

- **First discovery pays once globally.** Browser run, all 27 puzzles solved in order on a synthetic save: every word's coins and XP matched an independently computed expectation (7 coins per letter and round(1.5 × letters) XP once per term; 1 coin / 0 XP once per world for ordinary words) with **zero discrepancies**. - **Replay protection:** replaying a completed puzzle awards 0 coins and 0 XP.
