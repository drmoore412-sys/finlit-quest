# FinLit Quest — Financial Vocabulary and Quiz Quality Standard

**Confirmed by owner:** 2026-10-17  
**Applies to:** Every proposed quiz created after the active 27-quiz baseline  
**Authority:** Product and puzzle-quality governance  
**Implementation status:** This standard does not approve or activate any quiz

## Product principle

FinLit Quest teaches real financial skills through increasingly challenging word puzzles. Financial vocabulary must drive puzzle creation; letter compatibility must never drive the selection of educational content.

## Mandatory acceptance rules

Every new quiz must:

1. Contain at least **3 required words**.
2. Prefer **4–5 required words** to provide appropriate challenge.
3. Contain at least **3 approved financial terms**.
4. Select financial terms for educational value, not merely because their letters fit.
5. Prioritize important approved financial terms that do not already appear in the active quizzes.
6. Use only approved playable vocabulary. Any new financial concept must remain a research candidate pending explicit owner review.
7. Preserve every existing permanent term ID, exact approved definition, mastery identity, and owning world.
8. Pass the complete strict crossword validation suite.
9. Have an answer composition that does not duplicate an active quiz or another proposal.
10. Include an educational comprehension question that tests how at least one financial concept works.
11. Be rejected when it contains fewer than 3 required words or fewer than 3 approved financial terms.
12. Leave the existing 27 quizzes unchanged.

## Validation order

Each candidate must pass these gates in order:

1. **Vocabulary authority:** Every financial term resolves to an approved playable record. New concepts are flagged and excluded from playable proposals.
2. **Educational review:** The selected terms form a meaningful learning experience and the comprehension question tests application or understanding.
3. **Composition requirements:** The candidate contains 3–5 required words and at least 3 approved financial terms; 4–5 words are preferred.
4. **Exact-wheel validation:** Every required answer can be formed from the anchor's exact letter multiset.
5. **Strict crossword validation:** Crossing letters, separation, slot boundaries, contained words, connectivity, and engine acceptance all pass.
6. **Duplicate rejection:** The normalized answer set is unique across active quizzes and pending proposals.
7. **Owner review:** The owner approves, revises, or rejects the composition and comprehension question.
8. **Separate implementation authorization:** Approval does not by itself authorize export or activation.

Candidates that fail any mandatory gate are ineligible for ranking or owner approval. Automatic repair, when available, must create a new candidate and rerun the entire validation sequence.

## Protected baseline

The active 27 quizzes, their IDs, compositions, rewards, term identities, saves, mastery records, and player progress are protected. Expansion work must be additive and remains outside the playable game until separately authorized.
