### Evidence supporting the batch

- REPORT and HISTORY are directly defined by the [CFPB mortgage key terms](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/mortgages/answers/key-terms/).
- BUREAU is explicitly described as another name for a credit reporting company by the [CFPB](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/archive/newsroom/consumer-financial-protection-bureau-report-details-how-the-nations-largest-credit-bureaus-manage-consumer-data/) and is used throughout the [FTC credit-report guidance](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/free-credit-reports).
- FREEZE has a current term-specific [CFPB definition and consumer-protection explanation](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-is-a-credit-freeze-or-security-freeze-on-my-credit-report-en-1341/).
- ATM is directly defined in the [CFPB youth financial glossary](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/educator-tool/youth-financial-education/glossary/) and described by the [FDIC](https://www.fdic.gov/consumer-resource-center/deposit-accounts).
- WIRE is supported by the [Federal Reserve's wire-transfer definition](https://www.federalreserve.gov/frrs/regulations/commentary-on-section-2292-definitions.htm).
- PHISHING is directly supported by the [FTC's phishing guidance](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/how-recognize-avoid-phishing-scams).
- ADDRESS and SCAM are directly supported by the [FTC cryptocurrency guidance](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/what-know-about-cryptocurrency-scams).
- CUSTODY is supported by the [CFTC digital-asset terms guide](https://www.cftc.gov/sites/default/files/2022-09/KeyTerms.pdf), which distinguishes custodial and noncustodial wallets.
- CONFIRMED matches the [NIST CSRC glossary](https://csrc.nist.gov/glossary/term/confirmed), unlike the package's verb CONFIRM.
- LIQUIDITY is directly defined by [Investor.gov](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/glossary/liquidity-or-marketability).
