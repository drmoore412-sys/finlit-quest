# FinLit Quest 2027 — Thirteen-World Expansion

**Date:** 2026-10-18

**Status:** Eight new world identities recorded; vocabulary and puzzle proposals prepared; runtime implementation pending curriculum approval

## Executive result

FinLit Quest now has a governed 13-world product definition: five currently playable worlds and eight owner-approved future playable worlds. The eight new worlds are currently **vocabulary-only** because proposed terms and puzzles are not implemented or definition-approved. The existing 27 puzzles remain unchanged.

The consolidated queue contains 57 unique puzzle proposals. If every proposal were later approved and implemented, the game would contain 84 puzzles. No world yet reaches the 15-puzzle minimum under the quality rules, so exact shortfalls are preserved rather than filled with artificial terminology.

## Flashcards and study preservation

All 376 world associations for 10+ letter terms are retained in the study package. Counts are associations, not globally unique terms; one cross-world term may appear in more than one world. Short terms are also preserved. No term was shortened to fit a crossword, and no flashcard feature was implemented.

## Implementation blockers

1. The eight world identities are owner-approved, but most terms still lack owner-approved definitions, permanent IDs, clues, and mastery ownership.
2. No new world has an approved runtime puzzle bank; proposed puzzles must not be counted as implemented.
3. Each world remains below the 15-puzzle minimum under the exact-nine-letter and financial-quality rules.
4. AI & Technology has only one qualifying nine-letter composition in the current financial vocabulary; platform and tool names cannot substitute for three financial terms.
5. Social-media platform names are contextual brands, not financial concepts. The Digital Income curriculum needs term-level definitions and more compatible nine-letter anchors.
6. Runtime cards, saves, navigation, and iOS bundling should be extended only after a new world's term registry and first puzzle bank are approved together.

## Files and restrictions

The world definitions, vocabulary index, consolidated proposal package, study package, and this report are data/research artifacts only. Existing gameplay, navigation, active puzzles, rewards, saves, and native bundles were not changed.
