## Validation interpretation

Every new candidate passes the exact nine-letter inventory check, 4–9-letter answer rule, strict crossword placement validation, separation rules, contained-word rule, and duplicate-composition checks. Every required answer is drawn from the integrated financial-literacy vocabulary master.

A structural PASS is not curriculum approval. Working 2027 vocabulary retains a research key and draft clue; it receives no permanent ID. Exact owner-approved definitions are reused only when a permanent identity already exists. Owner approval is required before any candidate can be exported to runtime.
